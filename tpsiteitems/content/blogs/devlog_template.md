# Fly Fast. Hit Hard. Build Better.

The core of the gameplay is complete. Our tagline captures the idea behind the gameplay loop. Fly Fast. Hit Hard. Build Better.
The game is designed to be fast paced, high mobility pvp. It's a 2D, top down shooter inspired by platform fighting games.
Think Super Smash Bros as a shooter in space.

## Hit Hard - Visually Striking Combat

"Sci-fi themes allow us to explore bright, colorful gameplay so long as combat can remain crisp and tactical."

You can afford 394 characters. high mobility pvp. It's a 2D, top down shooter inspired by platform fighting games. Describe high mobility design. 
Focus on feel good movement. Variable mobility. A focus on both something that feels realistic for space flight and good for gameplay. 
thruster controls, drift controls, lock and look controls. 3 Different Boost states. Weapon knockback etc etc.

I love bright, colorful games. I especially love bright, colorful sci-fi games. As a huge fan of games like No Man's Sky, Outer Worlds, Helldivers 2 and so many more,
I really wanted the game, despite it's top-down 2D style, to feel incredibly aesthetically appealing and cinematic. 
Earlier designs of the game had a more cyclical gameplay loop. With very quick deaths and very flat combat. But it took away the player's ability to enjoy
both the feel good movement and the various visually enjoyable elements. But I didn't want to take away the feel of heavy hitting weapons either. The rechargeable
shield system helped with this. Players now need to break through multiple layers of shields to eliminate and enemy while managing their own shield health and
flying their ship.
